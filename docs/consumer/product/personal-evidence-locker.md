# Personal Evidence Locker — Product Definition

## Purpose

The Personal Evidence Locker is a secure personal repository for documents and evidence that matter in everyday Canadian administrative life. It helps users preserve original records, organize them around situations and people, build a chronological record, and identify what may be missing — while keeping the distinction between source evidence and interpretation absolutely clear.

---

## Core proposition

- **Preserve originals**: uploaded files are kept bit-for-bit and never overwritten.
- **Organize around a situation**: evidence is grouped into cases or situations tied to a life event (job loss, tenancy dispute, consumer complaint, estate matter, etc.).
- **Build a timeline**: events, documents, and notes form a chronological record.
- **Separate fact from interpretation**: AI can suggest what a document might mean, but that interpretation is always labeled, stored separately, and never replaces the original.
- **User-controlled**: the user decides what to upload, how to label it, who it concerns, and whether to share or delete it.

---

## What it is

- A personal document and evidence repository.
- A place to organize evidence by situation, person, date, and category.
- A tool to build a private timeline of events with source references.
- A source of supporting material for Canadian Life Admin workflows and Canadian Document Generation.

## What it is not

- **Not a court-certified evidence-management system.** It is not certified by any court or tribunal.
- **Not a law-enforcement evidence repository.** It is for personal use and professional handoff, not for police or regulatory investigations.
- **Not a guarantee that stored evidence will be admissible in court.** Admissibility depends on many factors outside the product's control.
- **Not a legal-advice service.** It does not tell the user what evidence will win a dispute.
- **Not a backup service.** While the system should be reliable, users should maintain their own backups of critical documents.

---

## Evidence types

`PEL-REQ-001`: The system SHALL support, at minimum, the following evidence categories:

- contracts and agreements;
- leases and tenancy documents;
- employment documents (contracts, offer letters, termination letters, pay statements, ROEs);
- emails and letters;
- government correspondence;
- receipts and invoices;
- photos and screenshots;
- audio/video references where legally appropriate;
- insurance and warranty documents;
- incident records (notes, reports);
- documents generated by the user's other products in this family;
- other supporting evidence the user chooses to upload.

---

## Evidence ingestion

`PEL-REQ-002`: The system SHALL support ingestion methods:

- file upload (drag-and-drop and file picker);
- mobile capture (camera / scan);
- manual entry (typed note or summary);
- import from supported external sources (future; e.g., email import, cloud storage connector).

`PEL-REQ-003`: The system SHALL reject dangerous or unsupported file types (e.g., executables, scripts) and SHALL scan uploads for malware where technically feasible.

`PEL-REQ-004`: The system SHALL record the method of ingestion and the UTC timestamp of capture/upload for every item.

---

## Evidence organization

`PEL-REQ-005`: The system SHALL allow a user to organize evidence by:

- **Case / situation** — a grouping for a specific life event;
- **People / organizations** — parties involved (e.g., employer, landlord, service provider);
- **Dates** — event date, document date, received date, effective date;
- **Categories** — pre-defined and user-defined;
- **Tags** — user-defined labels;
- **Documents** — the original files and any generated derivatives;
- **Events** — discrete occurrences with dates and linked evidence;
- **Relationships** — links between evidence items, people, events, and cases.

`PEL-REQ-006`: A single evidence item SHALL be linkable to multiple cases, people, or events without duplicating the original file.

---

## Evidence analysis (conceptual)

`PEL-REQ-007`: The system MAY assist the user by suggesting structured information from evidence. Suggested analysis SHALL be stored as interpretation, not as primary evidence. It SHALL include:

- dates mentioned in the document;
- parties (people/organizations);
- amounts, currency, and payment references;
- obligations and deadlines;
- claims or statements made;
- contradictions or inconsistencies with other evidence;
- relevant communications that may relate to the matter;
- missing evidence the user may want to look for;
- chronology suggestions.

`PEL-REQ-008`: The system SHALL clearly label every machine-suggested interpretation as:

- generated by AI or another automated process;
- confidence level (e.g., high/medium/low, or a numerical confidence);
- model or analysis type (where AI is used);
- generated-at timestamp;
- editable/correctable by the user.

---

## Timeline

`PEL-REQ-009`: The system SHALL support a timeline view for every case/situation, ordered by event date (or best-known date).

`PEL-REQ-010`: Each timeline entry SHALL contain:

- event description;
- date and date uncertainty (exact, approximate, unknown);
- linked evidence items;
- source reference;
- user notes;
- confidence (confirmed vs inferred).

`PEL-REQ-011`: The user SHALL be able to add, edit, reorder, and remove timeline entries.

---

## Evidence integrity

`PEL-REQ-012`: The system SHALL preserve the original uploaded file in an unmodified form.

`PEL-REQ-013`: The system SHALL store metadata for every evidence item:

- original filename;
- file size;
- MIME type;
- cryptographic hash (e.g., SHA-256) of the original file;
- ingestion timestamp;
- ingestion method;
- provenance note (user-provided or imported source);
- user annotations.

`PEL-REQ-014`: The system SHALL support versioning for user-edited derivatives (e.g., redacted exports, summaries) without altering the original.

`PEL-REQ-015`: The system SHALL maintain an append-only audit history per evidence item and per case:

- create;
- view;
- download/export;
- link/unlink;
- annotation added or edited;
- interpretation generated or corrected;
- deletion (soft delete, with recovery window if supported).

`PEL-REQ-016`: The system SHALL record provenance clearly: who provided the evidence, when, and from what source.

`PEL-REQ-017`: The system SHALL separate source evidence from AI-generated interpretation so the two cannot be confused.

`PEL-REQ-018`: The user SHALL be able to add human-authored notes to any evidence item or timeline entry.

---

## Evidence vs AI interpretation

`PEL-REQ-019`: The product SHALL enforce a strict separation:

- **Primary evidence**: the uploaded file, its metadata, and user annotations.
- **AI interpretation**: machine-generated summaries, extracted dates/parties/amounts, contradiction flags, chronology suggestions.

`PEL-REQ-020`: AI interpretation SHALL be displayed alongside but distinctly from primary evidence, with clear provenance and confidence indicators.

`PEL-REQ-021`: The user SHALL be able to correct or dismiss any AI interpretation. Corrections SHALL be stored as user annotations, not as replacements for the original AI output.

`PEL-REQ-022`: Exported evidence packages SHALL distinguish originals, user annotations, and AI interpretations unless the user explicitly chooses to include only originals.

---

## Search and retrieval

`PEL-REQ-023`: The system SHALL support search across:

- file names;
- user annotations;
- tags;
- case/situation names;
- extracted text (if OCR is supported);
- people/organizations.

`PEL-REQ-024`: Search results SHALL indicate whether a match came from primary evidence or from AI interpretation.

---

## Export and portability

`PEL-REQ-025`: The user SHALL be able to export:

- a single evidence item (original + metadata);
- a case/situation package (originals, timeline, annotations);
- an immutable evidence summary (e.g., PDF report) suitable for handoff to a professional.

`PEL-REQ-026`: Exports SHALL include integrity metadata (hash, source, timestamps) and a clear statement that the export is user-provided, not court-certified.

`PEL-REQ-027`: The system SHALL NOT share evidence with third parties without explicit user action.

---

## Deletion and retention

`PEL-REQ-028`: The user SHALL be able to delete individual evidence items, cases, or their entire account. Deletion policy details are in `data/retention-and-deletion.md`.

`PEL-REQ-029`: The system SHALL support a recovery window for accidental deletion if the retention policy allows.

---

## Privacy and sensitivity

`PEL-REQ-030`: The system SHALL treat evidence as sensitive personal information by default.

`PEL-REQ-031`: The system SHALL provide tools to mark items as highly sensitive (e.g., health, financial, identity documents) and apply additional access controls.

`PEL-REQ-032`: AI analysis of sensitive evidence SHALL be optional and SHALL require explicit user consent per `privacy-requirements.md`.

---

## Integration with other products

`PEL-REQ-033`: The Evidence Locker SHALL be capable of receiving evidence references from Canadian Life Admin workflows and Canadian Document Generation without exposing unrelated evidence.

`PEL-REQ-034`: Generated documents from Canadian Document Generation MAY be stored in the Evidence Locker as generated documents, clearly labeled as such.
