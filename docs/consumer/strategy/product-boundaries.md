# Product Boundaries

This document states what each product is **not**. It must be reflected in user-facing copy, disclaimers, escalation flows, and support training.

---

## Canadian Life Admin

**Is not:**

- A law firm or a lawyer.
- A government agency.
- A financial advisor, tax preparer, accountant, or immigration consultant.
- An emergency service. For emergencies, users must contact 9-1-1 or the relevant crisis line.
- A substitute for regulated professional advice.
- A guarantee of any outcome, deadline, right, or entitlement.

**What it provides:**

- Plain-language administrative guidance.
- Pointers to authoritative sources.
- Structured next steps.
- A clear signal when a qualified professional or government office should be consulted.

---

## Canadian Document Generation

**Is not:**

- A law firm or legal representation service.
- A service that files documents with courts, tribunals, or government bodies.
- A notarization, commissioning, or certification authority.
- A substitute for a lawyer reviewing a user's specific facts.
- An automated system that can determine what is legally correct in a disputed matter.

**What it provides:**

- Template-based drafting of routine administrative correspondence.
- Structured questions that populate a document.
- A preview and review step before the user decides to use the document.
- Clear flags when a document should be reviewed by a qualified professional.

---

## Personal Evidence Locker

**Is not:**

- A court-certified evidence-management system.
- A law-enforcement evidence repository.
- A guarantee that stored evidence will be admissible in court.
- A legal-advice service about what evidence will win a dispute.
- A permanent backup or disaster-recovery service.

**What it provides:**

- A secure personal repository for documents and records.
- Tools to organize, search, and build a timeline.
- Integrity metadata (hashes, provenance, audit history).
- A clear distinction between original evidence and machine-generated interpretation.

---

## Shared boundaries

These apply to all three products:

1. **Not legal advice.** No output should be read as advice about what the user should do in their specific legal circumstances. Where the matter is high-risk or fact-dependent, the product must recommend a qualified professional.
2. **Not a government filing service.** The product may help prepare correspondence or supporting documents; it does not submit them.
3. **Not a regulated professional.** The product does not hold professional insurance, owe fiduciary duties, or create solicitor-client, consultant-client, or agency relationships.
4. **Not infallible.** Jurisdiction, law, and personal circumstances change. The product must be transparent about uncertainty and provide sources for verification.
5. **Not a data broker.** User data and documents are not sold, used for advertising, or mined for unrelated purposes.

---

## How the boundaries show up in the product

- **Disclaimers** appear on substantive guidance surfaces, generated documents, and exports.
- **Escalation** flows route users to professionals or emergency services when appropriate.
- **Confidence indicators** explain when the system is sure and when it is not.
- **Source links** point users to official legislation and agencies for verification.
- **Prohibited document categories** are blocked or flagged before generation.
- **Human review gates** exist for high-risk documents and situations.
- **Support responses** reinforce the boundaries; support staff do not give legal, tax, immigration, or financial advice.
