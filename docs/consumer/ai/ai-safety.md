# AI Safety

## Design principle

The products use a generative model only where open-ended natural-language understanding is required. Around that generative core, a deterministic safety layer enforces rules that are cheap, auditable, and fail-safe. A safety rule can only tighten, never loosen, the model's output.

This mirrors Dutiva's deterministic safety backstop (`docs/AI_USAGE_STRATEGY.md` §5) but is scoped to consumer administrative situations.

---

## Safety backstop components

### 1. Crisis / distress intercept

`AI-REQ-042`: The system SHALL detect signals of imminent harm or severe distress from user input and SHALL respond with verified crisis resources, not administrative guidance.

`AI-REQ-043`: Crisis detection SHALL use a maintained, bilingual phrase set and SHALL be OR'd with any model classification. A rule-based detection can raise a crisis but cannot clear one.

```text
isCrisis_final = detectCrisisSignal(userText) OR model.isCrisis
// if isCrisis_final → crisis resources only; administrative guidance gated off
```

`AI-REQ-044`: Crisis resources SHALL be emitted verbatim from a verified list and SHALL NOT be generated by the model.

`AI-REQ-045`: Examples of crisis signals include explicit statements of self-harm intent, threats of violence, reports of immediate physical danger, or expressions of severe distress that indicate the user may not be able to act rationally.

`AI-REQ-046`: The system SHALL provide Canadian-specific resources where relevant (e.g., 9-8-8 Suicide Crisis Helpline, 9-1-1 for emergencies, local crisis lines). The exact resources SHALL be maintained by a human reviewer.

### 2. Jurisdiction gate

`AI-REQ-047`: The system SHALL default jurisdiction to `unknown` and SHALL NOT assume a province, territory, or federal scope to produce an answer.

`AI-REQ-048`: Until jurisdiction is confirmed, the system SHALL:

- present only jurisdiction-neutral guidance;
- withhold province/territory-specific statutory figures, deadlines, and citations;
- ask the user to confirm jurisdiction;
- display a warning if the model output mentions jurisdiction-specific figures.

`AI-REQ-049`: The system SHALL maintain a `jurisdiction.status` state machine with values: `unknown`, `suggested`, `confirmed`, `assumed`, `multi`, `not_applicable`.

`AI-REQ-050`: A jurisdiction suggestion based on clues (e.g., "I work for a bank" → federal) SHALL be presented for confirmation, not applied silently.

### 3. Statutory-figure gate

`AI-REQ-051`: The system SHALL withhold statutory figures (weeks, months, days, dollar amounts, percentages) when jurisdiction is not confirmed or when the underlying lookup table is not vetted.

`AI-REQ-052`: For situations where an encoded lookup table exists (e.g., a statutory notice schedule for a confirmed jurisdiction), the figure the model states SHALL be cross-checked against the table.

`AI-REQ-053`: A mismatch between the model's prose and the deterministic table SHALL:

- withhold the authoritative legal basis;
- display a warning to verify any figure in the reply;
- not rewrite the model's prose silently.

`AI-REQ-054`: Where no encoded table exists, the system SHALL say so rather than guess, and SHALL direct the user to the official source.

### 4. Professional-advice / escalation gate

`AI-REQ-055`: The system SHALL detect high-risk or regulated-professional situations and SHALL present an escalation banner with a recommendation to consult a qualified professional.

`AI-REQ-056`: High-risk triggers include:

- allegations of discrimination, harassment, or reprisal;
- litigation or threatened litigation;
- criminal allegations or fraud;
- immigration, tax, or family-law matters;
- complex multi-jurisdiction issues;
- situations where a statutory deadline may have passed;
- matters where a wrong action could cause significant financial or legal harm.

`AI-REQ-057`: The escalation gate SHALL still provide safe, immediate next steps (e.g., preserve evidence, document dates, contact the appropriate agency) while recommending professional help.

### 5. Evidence integrity gate

`AI-REQ-058`: The system SHALL NOT allow a model output to alter, delete, or overwrite original evidence.

`AI-REQ-059`: Any model-generated extraction or summary SHALL be stored as `EvidenceInterpretation`, not as a replacement for the original file or user annotation.

`AI-REQ-060`: When a model output is used to suggest edits to a generated document, the system SHALL require explicit user review and confirmation.

---

## Monotonicity

`AI-REQ-061`: Safety gates SHALL be monotonic: they can only tighten (raise crisis, withhold a figure, escalate) and never loosen a gate the model has closed.

`AI-REQ-062`: Each gate SHALL be unit-testable with fixture-driven tests, independent of the model.

---

## Telemetry and observability

`AI-REQ-063`: When a safety gate fires, the system SHALL log a telemetry event with the gate name, action taken, and metadata, without recording PII or message bodies.

`AI-REQ-064`: Telemetry SHALL enable product and safety teams to answer:

- How often does the crisis intercept catch something?
- How often is jurisdiction withheld because it is unconfirmed?
- How often is a statutory figure flagged or withheld?
- How often does a high-risk escalation trigger?
- Are there patterns of false positives or negatives?

---

## Safety-by-design for new AI features

Before adding a generative model to any new feature, it must pass the following gate test:

1. **Open vocabulary?** Is the input genuinely open-ended natural language? If a fixed set of options or a lookup covers it, use those.
2. **Reproducibility.** Does the output need to be identical every time (documents, figures, deadlines)? If yes, it is deterministic, not generative.
3. **Grounding.** Are facts/citations retrieved and vetted, never recalled from the model?
4. **Fail-safe gate.** Is there a deterministic guard that fires on the safe side regardless of what the model returns?
5. **Auth + budget.** Is the call authenticated, rate-limited, and logged without PII?

A feature that cannot answer yes to 2–5 should ship deterministic first.

---

## Abuse and misuse prevention

`AI-REQ-065`: The system SHALL rate-limit and meter AI usage per user and per platform to prevent abuse.

`AI-REQ-066`: The system SHALL monitor for patterns of misuse (e.g., attempts to generate harassing, fraudulent, or deceptive documents) and SHALL refuse such requests.

`AI-REQ-067`: Refusals SHALL be logged but SHALL NOT store the harmful content beyond what is needed for safety review.

---

## Incident response

`AI-REQ-068`: The system SHALL have a documented process for handling AI safety incidents, including:

- reported harmful or incorrect outputs;
- suspected model hallucinations affecting users;
- abuse of the system to generate prohibited documents;
- data leakage via prompt injection.

`AI-REQ-069`: Safety incidents SHALL be triaged, reviewed, and used to update phrase sets, rules, or the knowledge base as appropriate.
