# Onboarding Checklist — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**New hire:** [Name]  ·  **Role:** [Title]  ·  **Start:** [Date]
**Manager:** [Name]

## Before day one (manager)

- [ ] Signed offer letter, employment agreement and confidentiality/IP agreement
      on file
- [ ] Job description (`05`) attached to the agreement
- [ ] Accounts and access requested: email, workspace, tools listed in the job
      description
- [ ] Equipment prepared or shipped (laptop, credentials, per `18`)
- [ ] Team notified of start date and role
- [ ] First-week schedule and buddy/contact assigned

## Day one (employee + manager)

- [ ] Government ID / work-eligibility document verified
- [ ] `06-new-hire-information-form` returned with TD1, TD1ON, banking, emergency
      contact
- [ ] Workspace and equipment walkthrough; security basics from `18` reviewed
      (passwords/MFA, device encryption, clean desk)
- [ ] Harassment & violence policy (`12`) and health & safety policy (`13`)
      reviewed — Ontario-required training logged
- [ ] Accessibility policy (`14`) reviewed — including how to request
      accommodation
- [ ] First assignments and 30-day expectations set

## First week

- [ ] Code of conduct (`11`), privacy policy (`15`), remote-work/IT policy (`18`)
      read
- [ ] Hours, overtime and vacation policy (`16`) and leaves policy (`17`) read
- [ ] `09-policy-acknowledgment` signed and filed
- [ ] Role-specific training started; recurring 1:1 scheduled

## First month

- [ ] First-month check-in held; blockers and questions addressed
- [ ] Probation review scheduled (start + 3 months) — `08-probation-review-form`
- [ ] Access permissions audited: least-privilege confirmed
