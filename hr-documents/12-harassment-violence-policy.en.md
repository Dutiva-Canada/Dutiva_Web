# Workplace Harassment & Violence Policy and Program — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Reviewed annually** (OHSA s. 32.0.1(1)(c))
**Applies to:** everyone in the workplace — employees, contractors, visitors.

Ontario's Occupational Health and Safety Act requires every employer to maintain
a workplace harassment policy and program and a workplace violence policy and
program. This document is ours.

## 1. Statement of commitment

Dutiva Canada Inc. will not tolerate workplace harassment or violence. Reports
are investigated promptly and fairly, and no one is penalized for reporting in
good faith.

## 2. Definitions (OHSA)

- **Workplace violence:** the exercise or attempt of physical force that could
  cause injury, and statements/behaviour a worker could reasonably interpret as
  a threat of physical force.
- **Workplace harassment:** vexatious comment or conduct known or reasonably
  known to be unwelcome — including sexual harassment, bullying, and
  harassment on Human Rights Code grounds. Reasonable performance management
  and direction are not harassment.

## 3. Reporting

Report harassment or violence to:

1. Your manager, **or**
2. Martin Constantineau, Founder & CEO — [email/phone], **or**
3. Any other person in authority you are comfortable with.

Reports may be made by someone other than the person affected. If the person
named in the report is the contact, report to another channel. Emergencies:
call 911 first.

## 4. Investigation

1. The Company assesses the report promptly and, where warranted, appoints an
   impartial investigator — external if internal impartiality is not possible.
2. The complainant and respondent are informed of the investigation; both may
   respond; relevant witnesses and documents are reviewed.
3. Interim measures (schedule changes, separation, remote work) may apply while
   the investigation proceeds.
4. The complainant and respondent are informed in writing of the results and of
   any corrective action, per OHSA s. 32.0.7.

All reports are handled confidentially, subject to investigation needs and
legal obligations.

## 5. No reprisal

No reprisal for good-faith reporting, participating in an investigation, or
exercising OHSA rights. Bad-faith or fabricated complaints are a conduct issue
under `19`.

## 6. Domestic violence

If the Company becomes aware domestic violence may enter the workplace, it will
take reasonable precautions to protect the worker — contact points, security
measures, schedule or location adjustments, and referrals to support resources.

## 7. Program measures

- **Assessment:** workplace violence risks are assessed (and reassessed when
  the workplace changes), including risks of domestic violence entering work.
- **Training:** harassment/violence training at onboarding (`07`) and after
  material changes; how to report, and how investigations work.
- **Posting:** this policy is available to all workers at all times [location].
- **Annual review:** the policy and program are reviewed at least yearly and
  after any incident.

## 8. Emergency response

Immediate threat → 911, then notify the contact in s. 3. Provide first aid and
preserve the scene; the incident is documented and the program reviewed.
