# Accessibility Policy — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Reviewed:** annually, and when the Integrated
Accessibility Standards (IASR) change.

Dutiva Canada Inc. is committed to accessibility consistent with the
Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA) and the Ontario Human
Rights Code. This policy covers how we accommodate people — employees,
candidates and clients — and which IASR obligations apply at our size.

## 1. Commitment

We identify, remove and prevent barriers. Accommodation is the default
approach — we individualize, we don't apply blanket rules, and we preserve
dignity and confidentiality.

## 2. Recruitment and hiring

- Job postings and offers state that accommodation is available on request.
- Candidates who ask for accommodation are accommodated through selection and
  assessment, at our cost.
- Successful candidates are told about our accommodation approach before or
  with the offer.

## 3. Employment

- Employees may request accommodation at any time, to their manager or the CEO —
  no particular form is needed.
- We respond promptly, consult the requester, and implement a reasonable
  accommodation (schedule, workstation, tools, duties adjustment) unless it
  causes undue hardship — assessed per the Human Rights Code.
- For employees who ask, or where a disability is known: individualized
  emergency-response information and, where needed, an individual accommodation
  plan or return-to-work process (IASR s. 27–28).
- Performance management and career development take accommodation needs into
  account.

## 4. Accessible formats and communication

- On request, we provide information in accessible formats — large print,
  screen-reader-compatible documents, or the format the person needs — at no
  cost, in a timely way.
- Feedback on our accessibility is welcome and can be given to
  [contact / email]; complaints follow the same response standards as any
  employee concern.

## 5. Facilities and digital services

- Physical spaces we control meet accessibility requirements; accessibility is
  considered when choosing or changing offices.
- Our website and product aim for WCAG alignment and improve as we grow.

## 6. Training

Training at onboarding (`07`) covers the Human Rights Code's accommodation
duty and how requests work; it is refreshed when this policy changes.

## 7. Scope note

This policy is written to meet the IASR obligations that apply to a small
organization and will scale as the team grows — including a multi-year
accessibility plan and written individual accommodation processes when headcount
triggers require them.
