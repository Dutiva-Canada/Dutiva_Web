# Code of Conduct — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Applies to:** all employees, contractors, and
anyone acting on the Company's behalf.

## 1. Purpose

This code sets the behavioural baseline for working at Dutiva Canada Inc. It
works alongside the employment agreement (`02`), the harassment & violence
policy (`12`) and the privacy and IT policies (`15`, `18`).

## 2. Professional standards

- Treat colleagues, clients, vendors and the public with respect and honesty.
- Do the work diligently and to a professional standard; flag mistakes early.
- Follow lawful and reasonable instructions; raise concerns rather than ignore
  them.
- Represent the Company accurately — no exaggerated claims about the product or
  its compliance posture, internally or externally.

## 3. Conflicts of interest

- Disclose outside work, investments or relationships that could conflict with
  Company interests or duties.
- Do not solicit Company clients or staff for a competing venture, during or
  outside work.
- Gifts: nominal-value hospitality is acceptable; anything that could influence
  a decision must be declined or disclosed.

## 4. Confidentiality and data

- Handle Company, client and personal information per `04-confidentiality-ip`
  and `15-privacy-information-policy`.
- Access only what the work requires; share only with those who need it.
- Report suspected breaches or leaks immediately (`18` for security incidents).

## 5. Respectful workplace

- No harassment, discrimination, violence, bullying or retaliation — see `12`
  for definitions, reporting and investigation.
- Accommodate human-rights and accessibility needs per `14` and the Ontario
  Human Rights Code.
- Debate ideas, not people; disagreement is fine, contempt is not.

## 6. Company property and time

- Use Company equipment, funds and systems responsibly and per `18`.
- Working time is for Company work unless arrangements say otherwise.

## 7. Breaches

Breaches are addressed per the progressive-discipline approach in `19`, up to
and including termination. Nothing in this code limits rights under the ESA,
OHSA, the Human Rights Code or any other legislation.
