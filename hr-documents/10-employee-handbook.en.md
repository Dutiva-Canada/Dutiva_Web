# Employee Handbook — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Version:** [1.0]  ·  **Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Owner:** Martin Constantineau, Founder & CEO

## Welcome

This handbook is the central reference for working at Dutiva Canada Inc. It
explains how our policies fit together and where to find each one. It is a
guide, not a contract — your employment agreement (`02`) governs your
employment terms, and legislation (Ontario's Employment Standards Act, the
Occupational Health and Safety Act, the Human Rights Code and others) prevails
over anything here.

## Who we are

Dutiva Canada Inc. builds HR-compliance software for Canadian employers. We are
a small team; that means each person's work matters directly, and our policies
are deliberately lean — enough structure to be fair, safe and lawful, without
bureaucracy.

## Day-to-day expectations

- **Do the work with care.** We ship compliance software; accuracy is the
  product.
- **Communicate plainly.** Raise blockers early; document decisions.
- **Protect information.** Client and employee data is confidential by default
  (`15`, `18`).
- **Ask.** If a policy is unclear, ask your manager before acting.

## Employment basics

- **Pay:** [bi-weekly], direct deposit; statutory deductions apply (`19`).
- **Hours:** normal schedule [40] h/week (`16`); overtime rules per the ESA.
- **Vacation:** per your agreement and `16`, never below ESA minimums.
- **Leaves:** all ESA job-protected leaves are available (`17`).
- **Reviews:** introductory review at 3 months (`08`), then annual (`19`).

## Raising concerns

- Work/conduct concerns → your manager, or the CEO directly.
- Harassment or violence → any channel in `12`, including outside your reporting
  line.
- Safety → follow `13`; report hazards immediately.
- Accommodation → `14`; ask — no formality needed to start the conversation.
