# Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Between** Dutiva Canada Inc. (the "Company") and **[Employee/Contractor name]**
(the "Recipient"), effective [date]. This Agreement forms part of the
employment agreement (`02`) or contractor agreement (`03`) between the parties.

## 1. Confidential information

1.1 "Confidential Information" means all non-public information of the Company,
including source code, product plans, pricing, customer and prospect lists,
personal information the Company holds, templates, prompts and workflows,
finances, and business strategy — whether disclosed before or after this
Agreement, and in any form.

1.2 Confidential Information does not include information that is or becomes
public through no breach by the Recipient, was already lawfully known to the
Recipient, is received lawfully from a third party, or is independently
developed without use of the Company's information.

## 2. Obligations

2.1 The Recipient will use Confidential Information only to perform services
for the Company; will not disclose it to anyone without a need to know who is
bound by similar obligations; and will protect it with at least reasonable care.

2.2 If compelled by law to disclose, the Recipient will, where lawful, give the
Company prompt notice and disclose only what is required.

2.3 These obligations continue after the working relationship ends, for as long
as the information remains confidential.

## 3. Intellectual property

3.1 "Work Product" means everything the Recipient creates, develops or
contributes to within the scope of services for the Company — software,
documents, templates, designs, data, inventions and improvements.

3.2 The Recipient assigns to the Company all right, title and interest in Work
Product, effective on creation. To the extent an assignment cannot be
immediate, the Recipient grants the Company a perpetual, irrevocable,
worldwide, royalty-free licence, and will sign further documents reasonably
needed to confirm the Company's rights.

3.3 To the extent permitted by law, the Recipient waives moral rights in Work
Product in favour of the Company.

3.4 Prior inventions are excluded only if listed in Schedule A. The Recipient
will not incorporate prior inventions into Work Product without written
consent.

## 4. Return of property

4.1 On request or at the end of the engagement, the Recipient will return or
securely delete all Company materials, devices, credentials and copies of
Confidential Information, and confirm deletion in writing if asked.

## 5. Remedies and general

5.1 The Recipient acknowledges breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable
harm for which damages are inadequate, and the Company may seek injunctive
relief in addition to other remedies.

5.2 Governed by the laws of Ontario and the applicable federal laws of Canada.

5.3 May be signed electronically and in counterparts.
