# Independent Contractor Agreement — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**This Independent Contractor Agreement** ("Agreement") is made between:

**Dutiva Canada Inc.** (the "Company") and
**[Contractor name / corporation]** (the "Contractor")

Effective **[Start date]**.

> **Status check first.** A contractor label does not override reality — if the
> relationship has set hours, ongoing direction, Company equipment and
> integration into the team, the law may treat the person as an employee with
> ESA entitlements and CRA payroll obligations. Use this only where the
> Contractor genuinely controls how and when the work is done. Have counsel
> confirm the status analysis before signing.

## 1. Services

1.1 The Contractor will provide the services described in Schedule A
("Services"), including the deliverables and milestones listed there.

1.2 The Contractor controls the manner, means, location and schedule of the
work, subject only to the deliverables and deadlines in Schedule A and to
reasonable coordination requirements.

## 2. Term

2.1 This Agreement runs from the effective date until [end date / completion of
the Services], unless ended earlier under section 9.

## 3. Fees and payment

3.1 The Company will pay the Contractor **[rate/fixed fee]** per
[hour/deliverable/milestone], invoiced [monthly] and payable within [30] days of
a correct invoice.

3.2 The Contractor is responsible for all taxes, HST (if registered), CPP
contributions, and any WSIB or equivalent premiums on their own account. No
source deductions will be made by the Company. If the Contractor is an HST
registrant, invoices must show the HST registration number and the tax charged.

3.3 Pre-approved expenses are reimbursed at cost against receipts, per the
expense terms in Schedule A.

## 4. Independent status

4.1 The Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Nothing in
this Agreement creates an employment, partnership, agency or joint-venture
relationship.

4.2 The Contractor receives no employee benefits, vacation pay, statutory-leave
entitlements or termination notice.

4.3 The Contractor may serve other clients, provided doing so does not conflict
with the Services or breach section 5.

## 5. Confidentiality and intellectual property

5.1 The Contractor's confidentiality and IP-assignment obligations are set out
in the Confidentiality and Intellectual Property Assignment Agreement
(`04-confidentiality-ip`), which forms part of this Agreement. All
deliverables, and the IP in them, vest in the Company on creation, subject to
payment of the associated fees.

## 6. Tools and access

6.1 The Contractor supplies their own equipment unless Schedule A states
otherwise. Any Company system access is governed by the IT acceptable-use
terms in `18-remote-work-it-policy`.

## 7. Warranties and liability

7.1 The Contractor warrants the Services will be performed in a professional
manner consistent with industry standards.

7.2 Each party's liability for claims arising from this Agreement is limited to
the fees paid or payable in the [six] months before the event, except for
breaches of section 5, fraud, or liability that cannot be limited by law.

7.3 The Contractor will carry the insurance (if any) listed in Schedule A.

## 8. Compliance

8.1 The Contractor will comply with applicable laws and, while using Company
systems or interacting with Company staff, with the conduct, harassment and
security expectations in `11-code-of-conduct` and `12-harassment-violence-policy`.

## 9. Termination

9.1 Either party may end this Agreement on [30] days' written notice.

9.2 The Company may end it immediately for material breach, breach of section 5,
or conduct inconsistent with section 8.

9.3 On termination the Contractor is paid for Services properly performed to the
termination date and returns or destroys Company materials and access.

## 10. General

10.1 This Agreement and Schedule A are the entire agreement for the Services.

10.2 Governed by the laws of Ontario and the applicable federal laws of Canada.

10.3 May be signed electronically and in counterparts.
