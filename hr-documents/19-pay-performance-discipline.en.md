# Pay, Performance & Discipline Policy — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]

## 1. Pay

- **Payday:** [bi-weekly, on (day)], by direct deposit. Pay stubs are provided
  each period.
- **Deductions:** statutory deductions only (income tax, CPP, EI) plus any
  authorized by you in writing or by law.
- **Questions:** pay discrepancies → [payroll contact] promptly; corrections are
  made on the next run.
- **Records:** payroll and employment records are kept per the ESA retention
  rules (`15`).

## 2. Performance management

- **Introductory review:** at 3 months (`08-probation-review-form`).
- **Annual review:** a written conversation each [year] — results against the
  job description (`05`), goals for the coming period, and development.
- **Day-to-day:** feedback is continuous — don't wait for the annual review to
  raise or receive concerns.
- **Goals:** set at the annual review (or hire); revisited at each 1:1 cycle.

## 3. Progressive discipline

Where conduct or performance falls short, the normal approach is progressive:

1. **Coaching/conversation** — informal; clarifies the expectation and the gap.
2. **Written warning** — the issue, the standard, what's expected, the
   consequence of recurrence, and the support offered. Filed.
3. **Final warning** — states plainly that further issues may end employment.
4. **Termination** — per `02` s. 8 and `20`.

Notes:

- Steps can be skipped where the matter is serious — serious misconduct,
  safety violations, harassment, or dishonesty can lead directly to final
  warning or termination for cause where the ESA standard is met.
- Discipline is proportionate, documented, and confidential.
- An employee may respond to any disciplinary record; responses are filed with
  it.

## 4. Compensation changes

- Salary reviews happen [annually]; changes are confirmed in writing.
- No employee's pay is reduced retroactively.
