# Privacy & Information Handling Policy — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Owner:** [Privacy contact — Martin Constantineau]

## 1. Scope

How Dutiva Canada Inc. collects, uses, stores, discloses and deletes personal
information — employee records first, plus the personal information we hold for
clients and contacts in the ordinary course of business.

## 2. What we collect and why (employees)

| Information | Purpose | Basis |
|-------------|---------|-------|
| Identity, SIN, tax forms (TD1/TD1ON), banking | Payroll, statutory remittances, ROE | Employment administration & legal obligations |
| Contact & emergency contact | Communication, emergencies | Administration |
| Performance, discipline, leave records | Managing the employment relationship | Administration & legal defence |
| Work-product and system-use data | Security, operations, `18` monitoring transparency | Legitimate operations |

Ontario has no general private-sector employee-privacy statute; we handle
employee information to PIPEDA-aligned standards anyway — collect what's
needed, use it for stated purposes, keep it accurate, protect it, and be open
about how.

## 3. Access and accuracy

- Employee records are accessible to the employee's manager and those who need
  them for payroll/HR. Need-to-know only.
- An employee may review their record and ask for corrections. Requests →
  [privacy contact].

## 4. Retention and disposal

- Employee records: kept for the employment period plus the statutory retention
  periods that apply (e.g., payroll and ROE retention requirements), then
  destroyed securely.
- No speculative retention — information we no longer need is deleted or
  destroyed per the retention schedule [reference/location].

## 5. Disclosure

Personal information is disclosed only:

- As the law requires (CRA, courts, Ministry of Labour, WSIB).
- To service providers bound to protect it (payroll, benefits, cloud).
- With consent, where the law allows it.
- In an emergency threatening life or safety.

## 6. Security

Access controls, encryption and least-privilege per `18-remote-work-it-policy`.
Suspected breaches are contained and reported per the incident process — to the
privacy contact immediately.

## 7. Client and contact data

Client-side personal information (HR data entered into the Dutiva platform,
business contacts) is handled under our client agreements and the same
care standard — minimized, protected, never sold, used only to deliver the
service.

## 8. Contact & complaints

Questions or privacy complaints → [privacy contact]. Unresolved concerns may
be raised with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada where PIPEDA
applies.
