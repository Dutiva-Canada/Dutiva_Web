# Remote Work & IT Acceptable-Use Policy — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Applies to:** employees and contractors with
Company system access.

## 1. Remote work

- **Eligibility:** remote/hybrid arrangements are set in the employment
  agreement (`02` s. 1.2) or agreed with the manager. The Company may require
  on-site presence for specific needs with reasonable notice.
- **Availability:** be reachable during your working hours; keep status and
  calendar current; let your manager know when you're stepping away.
- **Workspace:** a safe, ergonomically reasonable workspace per `13`; a quiet,
  private setting for confidential calls.
- **Performance:** remote work changes the where, not the what — the same
  output and conduct standards apply.

## 2. Equipment

- Company-issued devices are primarily for work; reasonable incidental personal
  use is fine.
- Personal devices may be used for Company work only where configured with the
  required security (encryption, screen lock, MFA).
- Care for equipment; report loss, theft or damage immediately — especially
  anything holding Company or client data.

## 3. Security

- **Authentication:** unique accounts, password manager use, MFA wherever
  offered. No credential sharing — ever.
- **Data:** Company and personal information lives in Company systems, not
  personal accounts or local files (`15`). Client data is handled only inside
  authorized systems.
- **Networks:** use trusted networks; VPN where required. No company work on
  public networks without protection.
- **Updates & encryption:** devices run supported OS versions, current patches,
  disk encryption and screen lock.
- **AI & third-party tools:** no Company confidential or personal information
  into unapproved external tools/AI services; use the approved toolset.
- **Incidents:** suspect a breach, phishing, lost device, wrong-person email →
  report immediately to [security contact]. Quick reporting is praised; hiding
  an incident is the discipline issue.

## 4. Acceptable use

- Systems are for Company work and lawful use. No harassment, illegal content,
  unlicensed software, or activity that would embarrass the Company.
- Reasonable personal use is permitted if it doesn't interfere with work or
  create risk.

## 5. Privacy and monitoring transparency

- Company systems may log and monitor use for security and operational
  purposes. Assume activity on Company systems is visible to administration;
  don't store personal content you expect to stay private on Company devices.
- Monitoring is proportionate: we don't surveil personal devices or personal
  accounts, and reviews of logs are need-based, not routine browsing.

## 6. On exit

All Company equipment, credentials and data are returned or destroyed at the
end of the engagement per `04` and `20`.
