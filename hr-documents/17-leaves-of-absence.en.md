# Leaves of Absence Policy — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Basis:** Ontario ESA, Part XIV — job-protected
leaves.

## 1. Overview

Employees are entitled to the job-protected leaves below under the ESA. Your
employment continues during a leave — you earn service, and benefits continue
where applicable. Lengths and eligibility below are ESA minimums; where a leave
is partially paid by statute, that is stated.

## 2. Statutory leaves (Ontario)

| Leave | Length | Paid? |
|-------|--------|-------|
| Sick leave | 3 days/year | Unpaid |
| Family responsibility leave | 3 days/year | Unpaid |
| Bereavement leave | 2 days/year | Unpaid |
| Family caregiver leave | 8 days/year | Unpaid |
| Family medical leave | Up to 28 weeks | Unpaid (EI may apply) |
| Critical illness leave | Up to 37 weeks (child) / 17 weeks (adult) | Unpaid (EI may apply) |
| Domestic or sexual violence leave | Up to 10 days + 15 weeks | First 5 days paid |
| Pregnancy leave | Up to 17 weeks | Unpaid (EI may apply) |
| Parental leave | Up to 61/63 weeks | Unpaid (EI may apply) |
| Child-related leaves (death, disappearance, adoption) | Per ESA | Unpaid |
| Reservist leave | Per deployment/training | Unpaid |
| Declared emergency leave | Duration of emergency | Unpaid |
| Organ donor leave | Up to 13 weeks | Unpaid |

Details, eligibility and notice requirements are per the ESA; ask before taking
a leave so we can confirm the specifics and keep records straight.

## 3. How to request

1. Tell your manager as early as possible — leaves can begin before written
   notice in urgent cases (illness, emergency), but follow up in writing.
2. State the leave type, expected start and duration.
3. Provide supporting documentation where the leave type allows us to request
   it (e.g., medical evidence for certain leaves — we ask only for what the
   ESA permits).
4. Keep us updated on your expected return; confirm before returning.

## 4. Rights during and after

- **Job protection:** you return to the same or a comparable position.
- **Seniority & benefits:** service accrues; benefit continuation per plan rules.
- **No reprisal** for taking or planning a leave.
- **Vacation:** vacation time continues to accrue during statutory leaves
  (vacation pay accrual follows the ESA).

## 5. Other time off

Short personal appointments are handled flexibly with the manager — not every
absence needs a formal leave. Company-specific paid leave benefits beyond ESA
minimums, if any, are set out in your employment agreement.
