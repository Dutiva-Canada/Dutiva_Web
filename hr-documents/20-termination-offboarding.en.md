# Termination & Offboarding — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Basis:** Ontario ESA Part XV + the employee's
agreement (`02` s. 8).

Four pieces: the without-cause termination letter template (A), the resignation
acknowledgment (B), the offboarding checklist (C), and the Record of Employment
steps (D).

---

## A. Termination letter (without cause) — template

**[Date]**

**[Employee name]**
[Address]

Dear [Employee name],

This letter confirms that your employment with Dutiva Canada Inc. ends on
**[termination date]**.

In accordance with your employment agreement and the Ontario Employment
Standards Act, 2000, you will receive:

- **Notice:** [working notice to [date] / pay in lieu of notice equivalent to
  [X weeks'] pay]
- **Severance:** [if applicable — state amount or "not applicable"]
- **Vacation pay:** accrued, unused vacation pay will be paid
- **Benefits:** [benefits continuation as required by the ESA]
- **Final pay:** issued by the later of the next regular payday or 7 days after
  termination, per the ESA

**Return of property:** by [date], return all Company property — devices,
credentials, documents and copies of Confidential Information — per your
agreements (`04`, `18`).

Your continuing obligations under the confidentiality and intellectual-property
agreement remain in effect.

A Record of Employment will be issued to Service Canada per section D.

This decision is final. If you have questions about your entitlements, contact
[contact]. You may wish to seek independent legal advice before signing any
release.

Sincerely,

Martin Constantineau
Founder & CEO, Dutiva Canada Inc.

---

*For-cause terminations need a different letter stating the grounds — get
counsel sign-off before issuing one. Resignations use section B.*

## B. Resignation acknowledgment — template

**[Date]** — Dear [Employee name],

We acknowledge your resignation received [date], with your last day of
employment **[last day]**. Final pay (including accrued vacation pay) follows
the ESA timing above. Please complete the offboarding checklist (section C)
before your last day; the confidentiality obligations in `04` continue after
employment ends.

## C. Offboarding checklist

**Employee:** [Name]  ·  **Last day:** [Date]

- [ ] Resignation/termination confirmed in writing; last day set
- [ ] Final pay calculated (incl. accrued vacation, any notice/severance)
- [ ] **ROE issued** to Service Canada — see section D
- [ ] System access scheduled to end on the last day: email, workspace, tools,
      code repositories, admin consoles
- [ ] Equipment returned: laptop, peripherals, keys/cards, phones
- [ ] Company data returned or deleted from personal devices; written
      confirmation received (`04` s. 4)
- [ ] Work transitioned: in-flight items documented and handed off; clients
      notified where relevant
- [ ] Final expense report submitted; outstanding advances reconciled
- [ ] Employee record updated: termination date, reason code, ROE filed
- [ ] Exit conversation offered; feedback noted
- [ ] Payroll: direct deposit kept active until final payment clears

## D. Record of Employment (ROE)

- Issue the ROE through ROE Web within the Service Canada timeline — **5 days**
  after the pay period in which the interruption of earnings occurs (or earlier
  where required).
- Use the correct reason code (A for layoff/shortage, E for quit, M for
  dismissal, K for other).
- Keep a copy in the employee's record.

> *ROE timing is a statutory deadline — missed ROEs are the most common
> offboarding compliance failure. Set a calendar reminder the day the last day
> is set.*
