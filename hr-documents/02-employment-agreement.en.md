# Employment Agreement — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**This Employment Agreement** ("Agreement") is made between:

**Dutiva Canada Inc.** (the "Company"), an Ontario employer, and
**[Employee name]** (the "Employee")

Effective the **[Start date]**.

## 1. Position and duties

1.1 The Company employs the Employee as **[Job title]**, reporting to
[Manager name], performing the duties described in the attached job description
(`05-job-description`) and any related duties reasonably assigned.

1.2 The Employee's primary work location is **[location / remote within Canada]**.
The Company may adjust remote and on-site arrangements per its remote-work
policy (`18-remote-work-it-policy`).

1.3 The Employee will devote their working time, attention and best efforts to
the Company's business and will comply with the Company's policies as amended
from time to time, including those listed in the employee handbook
(`10-employee-handbook`).

## 2. Introductory period

2.1 The first three months of employment are an introductory period during which
fit and performance are assessed (see `08-probation-review-form`). This period
does not alter the termination provisions of section 8; statutory entitlements
apply from the first day of employment.

## 3. Compensation

3.1 The Company will pay the Employee a salary of **$[amount] per year**, less
applicable statutory deductions, paid [bi-weekly] by direct deposit.

3.2 Compensation is reviewed [annually]. Any change is confirmed in writing.

## 4. Hours of work

4.1 The normal schedule is [40] hours per week, scheduled per the hours-of-work
policy (`16-work-hours-overtime-vacation`).

4.2 If the Employee is eligible for overtime under Ontario's Employment
Standards Act, 2000 ("ESA"), overtime will be compensated in accordance with
the ESA and the hours policy. [If exempt (e.g., manager/supervisor or an
exempt professional category), state: "The position is exempt from ESA overtime
provisions as a [managerial/professional] role."]

## 5. Vacation and leaves

5.1 The Employee is entitled to **[X] weeks** of paid vacation per vacation year,
accrued and scheduled per the vacation policy, and never less than the ESA
minimums.

5.2 The Employee is entitled to all job-protected leaves under the ESA,
summarized in the leaves policy (`17-leaves-of-absence`).

## 6. Confidentiality and intellectual property

6.1 The Employee's obligations regarding confidential information and
intellectual property are set out in the Confidentiality and Intellectual
Property Assignment Agreement (`04-confidentiality-ip`), which forms part of
this Agreement. [Or: the same terms are set out in Schedule A.]

## 7. Policies

7.1 The Employee acknowledges receipt of, and agrees to comply with, the
Company's workplace policies, including the code of conduct (`11`), harassment
and violence policy (`12`), health and safety policy (`13`), accessibility
policy (`14`), privacy policy (`15`) and remote-work/IT policy (`18`), as
amended over time. Policies do not form part of this contract and may change,
except where the ESA or other legislation provides otherwise.

## 8. Termination

8.1 **By the Employee.** The Employee may resign by giving [two weeks'] written
notice.

8.2 **Termination without cause.** The Company may terminate the Employee's
employment without cause by providing **only** the notice of termination (or pay
in lieu), severance pay (if any) and benefits continuation required by the ESA.
The parties agree this provision limits the Employee's entitlements to the ESA
minimums and that the ESA entitlement is inclusive of and exhausts any
entitlement to reasonable notice under common law.

8.3 **Termination for cause.** The Company may terminate without notice or pay
in lieu where grounds for willful misconduct, disobedience or willful neglect
of duty exist under the ESA.

8.4 **Survival.** Sections 6 (confidentiality/IP) and 8 survive termination.

> *The termination clause is the most litigated provision in Canadian
> employment agreements. Have counsel confirm 8.2 achieves enforceable ESA-only
> limitation for this hire before signature.*

## 9. General

9.1 This Agreement, with the documents it references, is the entire agreement
about the Employee's employment and replaces prior representations.

9.2 This Agreement is governed by the laws of Ontario and the federal laws of
Canada applicable there.

9.3 If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stays in force, and the
provision is read down only as needed to make it enforceable.

9.4 This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.
