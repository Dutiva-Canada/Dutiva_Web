# Hours of Work, Overtime & Vacation Policy — Dutiva Canada Inc.

**Effective:** [Date]  ·  **Basis:** Ontario Employment Standards Act, 2000
("ESA"), Parts VII, VIII, XI — hours of work, overtime, vacation.

## 1. Normal hours

- The normal full-time schedule is [40] hours per week, [Monday–Friday,
  flexible core hours / per the employee's agreement].
- The ESA caps and rules apply regardless: maximum hours per day/week, daily
  and weekly rest periods, and meal breaks. We do not schedule or permit work
  outside those limits without a compliant excess-hours agreement.

## 2. Overtime

- Employees covered by the ESA overtime rules earn overtime pay after the ESA
  weekly threshold, at the ESA rate.
- Overtime must be **approved in advance** by the manager except in genuine
  emergencies; approved overtime is always paid, and unapproved overtime that
  was worked is also paid (with a conversation about the process).
- Some roles are exempt from ESA overtime (managers/supervisors and certain
  professionals). Whether a role is exempt is stated in its employment
  agreement (`02` s. 4.2).
- Where the employee agrees in writing, overtime may be banked as paid time off
  at the ESA rate instead of paid out — taken at a mutually agreed time.

## 3. Flexibility

- Reasonable flexibility in start/end times is supported where the work allows —
  coordinate with your manager.
- "Always-on" is not the norm: messages outside another person's working hours
  do not expect an immediate reply.

## 4. Vacation

- Vacation entitlement is per the employment agreement (`02` s. 5), never below
  ESA minimums (two or three weeks depending on service, plus the corresponding
  vacation pay percentage).
- **Scheduling:** request vacation to your manager as early as practical; we
  approve barring genuine business conflict, and ESA scheduling rules apply.
- Vacation year runs [January–December / anniversary]. Carry-over: [rule —
  e.g., up to one week may carry over with approval; ESA minimum cannot be
  forfeited].
- Public holidays: Ontario public holidays are observed per the ESA
  (entitlement and holiday pay per the statute).

## 5. Recording time

- Employees record time worked [system/location] — accurate records are a legal
  requirement for both sides.
- If your records are wrong or time was missed, tell your manager promptly —
  corrections are routine, not a problem.
